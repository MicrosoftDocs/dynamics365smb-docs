# Use Business Central without Outlook
[!INCLUDE[prod_short](includes/prod_short.md)] has deep integration with Microsoft 365, and you can use [!INCLUDE[prod_short](includes/prod_short.md)] as your business inbox in Outlook. But if you don't have Outlook, you can work with [!INCLUDE[prod_short](includes/prod_short.md)] in the browser or on your mobile device.  

## Send email without Outlook
You can send documents such as invoices as email using your business email address. The **Set Up Email** assisted setup guide helps you connect an email account to Business Central without installing Outlook. The information you need depends on the email connector you select. For example, an SMTP account requires mail server settings. If you don't have this information available, contact your IT support staff. Learn more in [Set up email](admin-how-setup-email.md).
