Use the **Balance Sheet** report to view the company's balance sheet. This is a legal report that is required for auditing accounts. You can use this report to view assets and liabilities.
  
## Print a balance sheet report

1. [!INCLUDE [open-search](../../../includes/open-search.md)], enter **Balance Sheet**, and then choose the relevant link.  
1. On the request page, fill in the fields as described in the following table.  
  
   |Field|Description|  
   |---------------------------------|---------------------------------------|  
   |**Amounts in whole**|Specifies the unit used to round the amounts, such as tens, hundreds, or thousands.|  
  
   > [!NOTE]
   > On the **G/L Account** FastTab, set a **Date Filter** that ends on the balance sheet date. The report uses that date to calculate the current and previous fiscal year balances.

1. Choose the **Print** button to print the report or choose the **Preview** button to view it on the screen.
