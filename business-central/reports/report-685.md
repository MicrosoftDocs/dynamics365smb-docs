# Payment Practice (report)

The **Payment Practice** report summarizes how a company pays its vendors over a chosen reporting period. It shows the average agreed and actual payment periods, the percentage of invoices paid on time, and additional statistics such as median, mode, and percentile payment times, Peppol adoption, and small business payment shares. Depending on the layout selected, the report breaks these figures down by payment period, vendor company size, or small business status, giving a detailed view of payment practices at both the aggregate and line level.

You can filter the report by starting date and ending date to define the reporting period, by aggregation type to control how the payment statistics are summarized, and by header type to select the specific payment practice record to report on.

## Use cases

[!INCLUDE [report-685-scenario](../includes/report-685-scenario-include.md)]

Controllers and finance teams can use the report to:

* Review the average agreed versus actual payment period to see whether the company is paying vendors within agreed terms.
* Check the percentage of invoices paid on time for a selected period to monitor payment discipline.
* Use the median, mode, and percentile payment time figures to identify outliers and typical payment behavior.
* Produce the vendor size breakdown to compare payment performance across different vendor company sizes.,Compliance and reporting officers can use the report to:

* Generate the small business layout to document the percentage of payments made to small business vendors and Peppol-enabled transactions.
* Use the period layout to prepare statutory payment practice disclosures for a specific starting and ending date.
* Verify the payment period breakdown lines to support submissions required by payment practice reporting regulations.
