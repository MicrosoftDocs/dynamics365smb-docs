# Rounding account in inventory in the Czech version

The rounding account in inventory feature lets you post all rounded costs (rounding entries in the `Value Entry` table) to a general ledger account other than the inventory adjustment account. This feature lets you post a rounded cost to a different account than the acquisition cost.

## Set up the rounding account

1. Open the **General Posting Setup** page.
1. For the relevant combination of posting groups, enter an account in the **Invt. Rounding Adj. Account** field.
