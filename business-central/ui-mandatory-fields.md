# Identify mandatory fields

When you enter data, there might be a red asterisk next to a field. The asterisk highlights a field that you're expected to fill in for a certain process. For example, you're about to post a transaction, but you've not filled in an important field.

The visual clue doesn't force you to fill in the field. Whether leaving the field empty blocks a process depends on the validation for that process.

## Examples

On the **Customer Card** page, the red asterisk appears in the **Name**, **Gen. Bus. Posting Group**, and **Customer Posting Group** fields. In the North American version, it also appears in the **Tax Area Code** field. The asterisk itself doesn't prevent posting a sales transaction.

On the **Item Card** page, the red asterisk appears in the **Description** field. However, you can add the item to a sales order line by selecting its item number even if the description is empty.
