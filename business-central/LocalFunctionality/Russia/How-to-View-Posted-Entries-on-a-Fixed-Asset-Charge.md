# View posted entries on a fixed asset charge

You can view all posted fixed asset entries for each fixed asset charge code. The following procedure shows how to view the posted entries.

## Steps to view a posted entry on a fixed asset charge

1. Choose the ![Tell Me feature](../../media/ui-search/search_small.png "Tell me what you want to do") icon, enter **Fixed Asset Charges**, and then choose the related link.
1. Open the fixed asset charge that you want to review.
1. Choose **Ledger Entries**. The entries are filtered by **FA Charge No.** for the selected charge.
