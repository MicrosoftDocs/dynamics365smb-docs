# Vendor - Top 10 List (report)

The **Vendor - Top 10 List** report ranks vendors by purchase amounts or balances in local currency. Use **Show** to choose **Purchases (LCY)** or **Balance (LCY)**. Use **Quantity** to change the number of vendors in the list.

The largest amounts appear first. The report also compares the listed vendors with the totals for all vendors included by the filters.

> [!NOTE]
> This report is obsolete. We recommend the [Vendor - Top 10 List Excel report](report-4404.md) instead.
