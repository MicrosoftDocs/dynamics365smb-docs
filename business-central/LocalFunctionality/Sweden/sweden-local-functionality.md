## Localization architecture

Swedish localization is delivered through extensions on top of the W1 base application. SE Core provides Swedish reports and other local functionality. The Standard Import Export (SIE) extension uses the Audit File Export app for exports. Automatic account codes and EU third-party purchase transactions are provided by shared W1 extensions.
