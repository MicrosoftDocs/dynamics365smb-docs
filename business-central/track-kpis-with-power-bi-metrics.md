# Track your business KPIs with Power BI goals

If you use Power BI on [!INCLUDE[prod_short](includes/prod_short.md)] data, it's easy to track KPIs or metrics that are important to you.

By using goals in Power BI, you can curate your own metrics and track them against key business objectives, in a single pane. This feature enhances data culture by promoting accountability, alignment, and visibility for teams and initiatives within organizations.

Follow this four-step process to set up Power BI goals:

1. Create a scorecard in Power BI. Learn more in [Create scorecards and manual goals in Power BI](/power-bi/create-reports/service-goals-create).
1. Add the goals you want to track by connecting to your Power BI report on Business Central data. Learn more in [Create connected goals](/power-bi/create-reports/service-goals-create-connected).
1. Define automated status rules for your goals, if needed. Status rules update a goal's status; they don't configure alert notifications. Learn more in [Create automated status rules for goals](/power-bi/create-reports/service-metrics-status-rules).

    This step automates status updates based on rules that govern the goal. Rules trigger changes based on value, percentage of target met, date conditions, or a combination of the three, making the rules versatile. For connected goals, these status rules refresh every time the data in your scorecard refreshes.
1. Follow goals to get Teams notifications when a goal is updated. For email updates, create a scorecard email subscription. Learn more in [Follow your goals](/power-bi/create-reports/service-metrics-follow).

Learn more in [Get started with goals in Power BI](/power-bi/create-reports/service-goals-introduction).

> [!NOTE]
> The report selection in [!INCLUDE[prod_short](includes/prod_short.md)] lists Power BI reports, not standalone scorecards. Open standalone scorecards in the Power BI service.
