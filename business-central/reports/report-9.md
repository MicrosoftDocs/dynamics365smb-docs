# Trial Balance/Budget (report)

The **Trial Balance/Budget** report shows general ledger (G/L) accounts with debit and credit net change and balances for a period you specify. It compares actual amounts with budget amounts, not with the same period last year. The percentages show actual amounts divided by budget amounts, multiplied by 100.

You can filter the report by dimensions.

Use **Budget Filter** to select a budget and **Date Filter** to define the period. The budget balance column covers the fiscal year through the period end.

> [!NOTE]
> This report is obsolete from version 28.0 and is replaced by [Trial Balance/Budget Excel](report-4406.md).

## Use cases

[!INCLUDE [report-9-scenario](../includes/report-9-scenario-include.md)]

Use the report to assess how well you're following your financial plans and identify variances between actual and budgeted amounts. The report is useful for financial analysis and decision-making at the end of an accounting period or fiscal year.
