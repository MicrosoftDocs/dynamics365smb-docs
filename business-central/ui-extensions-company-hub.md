# The Company Hub extension for Business Central

The Company Hub extension provides a dashboard with summary data for companies that you connect to in [!INCLUDE [prod_short](includes/prod_short.md)] online, such as the many clients of an accountant. The home page displays financial key performance indicators (KPIs) and links that let you open the individual environments and companies.

The dashboard is a highly specialized Role Center for a better overview of your work.

To get started, connect the company hub to the companies that you work in. Learn more in [Add companies to your company hub](company-hub-add-company.md).

## Installing the extension

To use the extension, you need permission to use the company hub. Installation doesn't automatically assign permissions or replace your Role Center.

To use the company hub as your home page, select **Company Hub** in the **Role** field on the **My Settings** page. You can also open the **Company Hub** page directly. Learn more about permissions and setup in [Manage work across multiple companies in the company hub](company-hub.md).
