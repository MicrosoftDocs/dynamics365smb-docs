# Track document lines

You can view documents that are related to sales order lines and purchase order lines, including documents from archived order lines. You can track related documents such as shipments, receipts, invoices, and blanket orders. This information helps you identify documents used to process orders.

## To track documents related to a sales order line

The following procedure describes how to track from a sales order line. The steps are similar for purchase order and blanket order lines.

1. [!INCLUDE[open-search](includes/open-search.md)], enter **Sales Orders**, and then choose the related link.  
1. Open a sales order that you want to track from.
1. Select a line, and then choose the **Document Line Tracking** action.
1. On the **Document Line Tracking** page, select the document that you want to view, and then choose the **Show** action to see the related line.
1. To view the entire document for the selected document line, choose the **Show Document** action.
