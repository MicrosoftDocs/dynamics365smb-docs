# VAT overview in the Russian version

The Russian version of [!INCLUDE[prod_short](../../includes/prod_short.md)] includes VAT posting, settlement, and ledger features for sales and purchases.

## VAT calculation

Use **VAT Posting Setup** to specify the VAT calculation type, percentage, and posting accounts for each combination of VAT business and product posting groups. The Russian localization also provides settings for transit VAT, manual VAT settlement, VAT reinstatement, and inclusion in VAT ledgers.

Confirm the rates, exemptions, and reporting rules that apply to your transactions under current requirements. The available product settings don't establish which tax treatment is legally applicable.

## VAT payments

Use the **VAT Settlement Worksheet** and **VAT Settlement Journal** to prepare and post manual VAT settlement entries. Use VAT purchase and sales ledgers to collect reporting data.

Confirm payment and filing deadlines with the relevant tax authority. This article describes product functionality, not current statutory deadlines.
