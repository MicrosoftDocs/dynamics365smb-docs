# Fiscal denial list in the Italian version

This article provides information on how to set up fiscally denied countries/regions and how to report the list of vendors from those countries/regions.  

## Set up countries/regions on the fiscal denial list

1. [!INCLUDE [open-search](../../includes/open-search.md)], enter **Countries/Regions**, and then choose the related link.
1. Select the country/region, and select **On Deny List**.
1. Close the page.

## Print the list of fiscally denied vendors  

1. [!INCLUDE [open-search](../../includes/open-search.md)], enter **Denied Vendors List**, and then choose the related link.
1. Print this report.

The report lists vendors whose **Country/Region Code** matches a country/region with **On Deny List** selected. It includes their addresses, purchase amounts, and balances in local currency.
