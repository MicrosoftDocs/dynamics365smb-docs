# Internal financial documents

Users who perform general ledger operations need to print documents for these operations, with a layout that complies with legal requirements.

For this reason, this feature provides the following reports:

- **General Journal - Test CZ**: Prints documents from G/L journals.
- **General Ledger Document CZ**: Prints posted general ledger operations.
