# Check of output in inventory – time sequence in the Czech version

The standard functionality of [!INCLUDE[d365fin](../../includes/d365fin_md.md)] is extended by a check on the time sequence of warehouse operations. To turn on the check, select **Date Order Inventory Change** on the **Inventory Setup** page. Once it's on, you can't post an outbound item movement with a posting date earlier than the posting date of its related receipt.
