# Machine Center Load (report)

> [!NOTE]
> This report is obsolete and has been replaced by the [Work/Machine Center Load](https://businesscentral.dynamics.com?report=99000792) report.

The **Machine Center Load** report shows the workload on a machine center. The load on a machine center is the total allocated production order time in a specified period.
