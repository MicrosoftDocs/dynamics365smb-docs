# Accounting output documents in the Czech version

To comply with the legislation, reporting features, and local reporting practices of Czech companies, this feature provides the following reports:

- **General Journal**
- **General Ledger**
- **Accounting Sheets**
- **Turnover Report by Global Dimensions**
- **Open G/L Entries to Date**
- **Inventory Account to Date**
- **Joining Banking Account Adjustment**
- **Joining G/L Account Adjustment**
- **G/L VAT Reconciliation**
- **All Payments on Hold**
- **Open Customer Entries to Date**
- **Open Vendor Entries to Date**
- **Fiscal Year Balance** - standard report adjusted
- **Trial Balance by Period** - standard report adjusted
