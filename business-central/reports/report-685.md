# Payment Practice (report)

The **Payment Practice** report summarizes how a company pays its vendors over a specified date range. It shows the reporting period and aggregation type, along with key metrics such as the average agreed payment period, the average actual payment period, and the percentage of invoices paid on time. Depending on the selected layout, the report also includes additional detail such as median, mode, and percentile payment times, Peppol adoption, and payment breakdowns by vendor company size or payment period.

You can filter the report by starting date and ending date to define the reporting period, by aggregation type to control how payment data is summarized, and by header type to determine which payment practice header records are included in the report.

## Use cases

[!INCLUDE [report-685-scenario](../includes/report-685-scenario-include.md)]

Finance and compliance teams can use the report to:

* Produce statutory payment practice reports covering a defined reporting period.
* Verify the average agreed and actual payment periods before submitting external disclosures.
* Check the percentage of invoices paid on time to confirm compliance with payment policies.
* Review median, mode, and percentile payment times for a more detailed statistical picture.,Controllers and accounts payable managers can use the report to:

* Compare payment performance by vendor company size to spot inconsistent treatment of small suppliers.
* Track the percentage of small business payments to support supplier relationship reviews.
* Monitor Peppol-enabled payment usage across the reporting period.
* Break down payment periods by line to identify which payment terms are driving late payments.
