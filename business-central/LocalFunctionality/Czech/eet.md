# Registration of sales (EET) in the Czech version

> [!NOTE]
> The original Czech electronic registration of sales (EET) law was abolished on January 1, 2023, so businesses aren't currently required to register sales with the tax authorities. The Czech parliament has since passed a new law, known as EET 2.0, that's scheduled to take effect on January 1, 2027. The EET functionality described in this article remains available in the Czech version of [!INCLUDE[prod_short](../../includes/prod_short.md)] for customers who still need it.

Registration of sales (EET) is registration of sales coming from business activities and paid in cash. Information about these transactions is sent to the tax authorities. At the time of payment, a data message is created and sent online to the server of the tax office. The server responds with a message that contains a unique transaction ID, which must be printed on the receipt for the customer.

Payment methods included in EET:

- In cash
- By card
- Check
- Bill of exchange
- Other similar types, like gift cards, coupons, and bitcoin

Learn more at [www.etrzby.cz](http://www.etrzby.cz).

## How it works in [!INCLUDE[prod_short](../../includes/prod_short.md)]

[!INCLUDE[prod_short](../../includes/prod_short.md)] covers the following sales documents:

- Sales invoice payment
- Payment of prepayment invoice
- Refund sales credit memo
- Refund of prepayment invoice
- Cash desk receipt for sales to the G/L account (without a source sales document)

Posting one of these documents with a covered payment method creates an EET entry. Based on the sales regime, the entry is processed in one of these ways:

- **Online**: An EET entry is created and stored in [!INCLUDE[prod_short](../../includes/prod_short.md)]. A message is sent to the tax authorities' server, and the response is processed and stored. The customer's receipt shows the unique transaction ID generated by the tax authorities.
- **Offline**: An EET entry is created and stored in [!INCLUDE[prod_short](../../includes/prod_short.md)]. The customer's receipt shows a unique ID generated in [!INCLUDE[prod_short](../../includes/prod_short.md)] that identifies the company and document. EET entries are sent to the tax authorities later by a batch job.

## Main parts of the feature

- **EET Entries**: The page where registered documents are stored and processed. Each entry contains the sales data required by the tax authorities, for printing on receipts and for the electronic communication. Entries are created automatically when you post a covered source document.
- **EET Service Setup**: Settings for the connection to the tax authorities' server, the sales regime, and the certificate used to sign messages.
- **Certificate Codes**: Settings for the certificates used to sign EET messages.
- **EET Business Premises** and **EET Cash Registers**: Identification of the registered business premises and the cash registers (points of sale) within each premises.
