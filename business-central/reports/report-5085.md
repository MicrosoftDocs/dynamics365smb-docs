# Contact Cover Sheet (report)

The **Contact Cover Sheet** report prints a cover page that shows the contact's address and the company's address. It can also show the company's VAT registration number, giro number, bank name, and bank account number. You can run the report for one contact or for the contacts in a marketing segment, and optionally log the printing as an interaction.

You can filter the report by contact number to select specific contacts. When you run it from a marketing segment, you can filter by segment number to include the contacts linked to that segment. Turn on **Log Interaction** to create an interaction entry for each included contact.

## Use cases

[!INCLUDE [report-5085-scenario](../includes/report-5085-scenario-include.md)]

Sales and marketing teams can use the report to:

* Print a cover sheet for a contact before sending correspondence.
* Generate cover sheets for every contact in a marketing segment.
* Include the company's address and bank details without entering them manually.

Relationship managers can use the report to:

* Log that a cover sheet was sent to a contact.
* Include VAT registration and bank details in formal correspondence.
* Track the cover sheet in the contact's interaction history.
